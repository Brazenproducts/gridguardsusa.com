How Grid Guards® Work

Your spray paint booth filter grid takes a beating shift after shift. Overspray builds up. Cured paint locks onto the metal. Eventually your team is spending hours scraping grids clean instead of running production.

Grid Guards® sit on top of your grid as a sacrificial protective layer. When it's time for a filter change, you simply remove and replace the Grid Guard — no scraping required. The filter grid underneath stays clean and protected.

  • Drops in over your existing filter grid
  • Reusable through multiple cycles
  • Protects your grid investment
  • Cuts clean-up labor dramatically
  • Compatible with BowTie® and BLOX™ filter systems
